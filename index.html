KNOW THE RISKS

Why Safe E-Waste Recycling Matters

Learn about harmful substances found in electronic waste.

Lead is found in some electronic components and older circuit boards. Improper disposal can contaminate soil and water. Exposure may harm the nervous system, kidneys and other organs.

Mercury may be present in certain older displays, lamps and electronic devices. It can enter the environment through improper disposal and damage the nervous system and aquatic ecosystems.

Cadmium is found in some rechargeable batteries and electronic components. It can contaminate soil and groundwater and may cause kidney and bone damage after long-term exposure.

🌱 Be Responsible!

Never throw batteries and electronic devices into regular household garbage. Use authorized e-waste collection and recycling channels.
